---

## 1. CUNY AI INNOVATION FUND CONTEXT & SYSTEMWIDE VISION
This initiative is a recipient of CUNY’s landmark **$3 Million AI Innovation Fund**, sponsored by the **CUNY Office of Academic Affairs (OAA)** under Chancellor Félix V. Matos Rodríguez. The systemwide program supports campus-led initiatives that harness artificial intelligence to transform higher education, advance AI literacy, safeguard academic integrity, and promote equitable, responsible AI adoption.

Operating out of City Tech's **Center for Holistic Integration (CHI)**, the **Self-Aware Room** advances this CUNY-wide mandate by tackling a foundational question for modern higher education:

> **"What does it mean to collaborate with machine intelligence?"**

Rather than treating AI as an external utility or isolated chatbot, the initiative uses the **Balanced Blended Space (BBS)** theoretical framework to convert physical classrooms into **active, machine-mediated living laboratories** where human students and AI agents collaborate as equal partners across physical, virtual, and conceptual domains.

---

## 2. CORE AI INNOVATION & RESEARCH PILLARS
The project directly advances CUNY OAA Priority Areas **1 (AI Education & Literacy)**, **2 (AI in Courses)**, and **4 (Ethical & Social Implications of AI)** through three core technical and pedagogical innovations:

1. **The Semantic Engine (`l4a_semantic.py`):** A real-time context routing layer that parses raw physical telemetry (speech, depth sensing, gesture, biosignals) into normalized semantic vectors that both human participants and AI systems can interpret and negotiate in real time.
2. **Personalized & Localized LLM Research:** Investigates custom, self-hosted Large Language Models (LLMs) deployed on institutional GPU hardware. This enables specialized AI agent personas (Socratic, Supportive, Analytical, Skeptical) while maintaining 100% local data governance and strict **FERPA compliance**.
3. **Closed-Loop Environmental Feedback Loop:** The physical room dynamically responds to human interaction—capturing user presence and dialogue, processing it through multi-agent pipelines, and rendering real-time spatial audio, lighting, and short-throw projection updates back into the environment.

---

## 3. THREE-COURSE CUNY OER CURRICULUM ARC
To ensure institutional scalability across CUNY, the initiative develops and pilots three CUNY-ready Open Educational Resource (OER) courses:

* **Introduction to AI:** Foundational AI concepts and spatial computing literacy.
* **Collaborative AI:** Hands-on instruction positioning AI agents as active co-creators rather than static tools.
* **Societal and Ethical Implications of Data Science (SEID 2364):** An advanced ethics course examining bias, algorithmic fairness, transparency, and accountability within mediated multi-agent environments. *(Piloted internationally at Modul University Vienna).*

---

## 4. MULTI-GRANT LEVERAGE & INSTITUTIONAL IMPACT
The CUNY OAA AI Innovation Grant ($25,000) achieves maximum cost efficiency by piggybacking on existing high-level state and federal research infrastructure:

* **Department of Defense (DoD / AVMI / GSVI):** Integrates high-fidelity 32-channel spatialized audio rendering and live telemetry coupling funded via Congressional Appropriation / U.S. Army GVSC (WPI/City Tech award).
* **GRTI Infrastructure:** Leverages CUNY Research Technology Infrastructure-funded projection fabrics, scrims, and spatial surfaces.
