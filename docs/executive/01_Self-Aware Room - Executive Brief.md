## **1\. PROJECT VISION & CENTRAL RESEARCH QUESTION**

Operating out of City Tech's Center for Holistic Integration (CHI), the **Self-Aware Room** investigates a foundational question at the intersection of spatial computing, defense simulation, and higher education:

*"What does it mean to collaborate with machine intelligence?"*

Grounded in the **Balanced Blended Space (BBS)** theoretical framework, the project transforms physical spaces from static rooms containing software tools into **active, machine-mediated living laboratories**. Human cognitive agents and computational AI agents operate as equal, symmetrical partners across physical, virtual, and conceptual domains.

## **3\. CORE AI INNOVATIONS & RESEARCH PILLARS**

* **The Semantic Engine (l4a\_semantic.py):** A real-time context routing layer that parses raw physical telemetry (speech, depth sensing, gesture, biosignals) into normalized semantic vectors for multi-agent reasoning.  
* **Personalized & Localized LLM Research:** Investigates custom, self-hosted LLMs deployed on local institutional GPU hardware. Enables specialized agent personas (Socratic, Supportive, Analytical, Skeptical) while guaranteeing 100% local data governance and FERPA compliance.  
* **Closed-Loop Spatial Feedback Mechanism:** Real-time environmental feedback where human inputs alter the room's semantic state, triggering AI responses that dynamically update spatial audio, lighting, and short-throw projections.

## **4\. THREE-COURSE CUNY OER CURRICULUM ARC**

* **Introduction to AI:** Foundational AI literacy and spatial computing principles.  
* **Collaborative AI:** Pedagogy positioning AI agents as active co-creators rather than static utilities.  
* **Societal and Ethical Implications of Data Science (SEID 2364):** Experiential ethics course evaluating bias, algorithmic fairness, transparency, and accountability inside mediated multi-agent environments. *(Piloted internationally at Modul University Vienna).*
